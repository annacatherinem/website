About

Investments are the last step.

Most people think financial planning starts with choosing investments. In my experience, that is usually where it ends, not where it begins.

I'm a CERTIFIED FINANCIAL PLANNER® with five years of experience, working with salaried professionals and families in Kochi, across Kerala and beyond. The people I meet are not careless with money. They are busy, and nobody ever showed them how the pieces fit together: income and spending, savings, insurance, taxes, investments, retirement.

My work is to make that picture clear, and then help build the financial discipline to act on it. Client centric, and free of jargon.

I write a weekly financial planning series on LinkedIn, breaking one piece of the puzzle down at a time. Educational, never advice.

Alongside the CFP certification I hold an MBA in Financial Management from NMIMS, the QPFP® designation from Network FP, and NISM certifications.

Practice

Areas I work across.

Comprehensive planning

One written plan covering income, spending, goals and the gap between them, with the trade-offs made explicit rather than buried.

Goal-based investing

Every rupee assigned a job and a date. Goals set the time horizon; the time horizon sets the risk.

Asset allocation

Funds chosen against a client's goals and their tolerance for a bad year, not against last year's returns.

Portfolio reviews

Periodic reviews that check the portfolio still matches the plan, and correct the drift when it doesn't.

Retirement planning

Working out the number to aim for, the path toward it, and how income is drawn once the salary stops.

Insurance & risk management

Questions

Common questions.

What does a Certified Financial Planner do?

A CFP® professional looks at your whole financial life together: income, spending, savings, insurance, taxes, investments and retirement. The result is one written plan that shows how each decision affects the others. In India, the CFP certification is awarded by FPSB India after examinations, work experience and a commitment to its code of ethics.

Is a financial planner the same as a mutual fund distributor?

No. A distributor is paid commission on the products they sell. Financial planning starts from your goals and cash flow, and products come last, if at all. In India, investment advice for a fee can only be given by a SEBI Registered Investment Adviser.

When is a good time to see a financial planner?

Usually around a change: a first job, marriage, a child, a home loan, a job switch, an inheritance, or retirement coming into view. Many families in Kochi and across Kerala also plan around income from abroad, which adds its own questions about tax, insurance and where money should be kept.

What does a financial plan include?

A cash flow picture, an emergency fund, life and health insurance needs, each goal with its cost and date, how investments are allocated against those goals, tax efficiency, a retirement path, and basics like nominations and a will.
