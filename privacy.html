Privacy note

Updated 23 November 2026

This note explains what happens to your details when you use this website or subscribe to The Financial Planning Letter.

What I collect

If you subscribe, I collect your name, your email address and, if you choose to add it, your WhatsApp number.

How I use it

I use your email address to send the monthly letter. If you add a WhatsApp number, I may send you occasional updates there. I don't sell your details or use them for anything else.

Who else handles it

MailerLite stores subscriber details and sends the letter for me. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so those services may receive technical details such as your IP address when you visit.

How long I keep it

I keep your details until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete them.

Your choices

Every letter has an unsubscribe link. To see, correct or delete your details, write to me at anna@annacatherinem.in.
